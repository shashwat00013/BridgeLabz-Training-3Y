Welcome to Jaipur

A city of royal palaces, rugged forts, and a skyline painted in shades of terracotta pink — my favorite destination in India.

Majestic Forts

From the hilltop grandeur of Amber Fort to the imposing walls of Nahargarh, Jaipur's forts tell stories of Rajput valor. Walking through their courtyards at sunset, with the city sprawled below, is an experience that stays with you long after you leave.

Vibrant Markets

Johari Bazaar

Famous for traditional Rajasthani jewelry and gemstones.

Bapu Bazaar

The place for textiles, juttis, and colorful handicrafts.

Chandpole Bazaar

Known for marble idols and traditional artwork.

Local Flavors

No visit is complete without dal-baati-churma, pyaaz kachori, and a glass of cold lassi from a century-old shop tucked inside the old city walls.
