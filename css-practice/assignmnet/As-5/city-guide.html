Discover Jaipur

The Pink City of India

HeritageFoodNature

Royal Heritage

Explore grand forts, historic palaces, and streets filled with stories from Rajasthan's past. Jaipur blends traditional architecture with a lively modern culture.

Flavours of Jaipur

Sample local favourites such as dal baati churma, ghewar, and kachori. Traditional markets are also wonderful places to discover spices and sweets.

Open Skies & Gardens

Take a peaceful break in a garden or visit the hills around the city. Early mornings offer cooler weather and beautiful views.

Travel thoughtfully · Respect local culture and heritage
